Source

Freely available French motor third-party liability data (freMTPL2freq.parquet), as issued for the Deep Learning for Actuarial Modeling summer school, Milan, 2026. All 678,007 policies are held in the page at full precision; nothing is sampled.

Frequency is ΣClaimNb / ΣExposure, severity is ΣClaimTotal / ΣClaimNb, and burning cost is ΣClaimTotal / ΣExposure. Every panel aggregates under all active filters except its own, so a filtered panel keeps the context you selected it from. The dashed line is the whole-book value with no filters applied, so each bar reads as a position relative to the portfolio. Bins holding less than the exposure floor are drawn hollow and excluded from the vertical scale, because a frequency measured on a few exposure-years is noise. Severity is dominated by a handful of very large claims (the largest single loss is €4.08m against a book mean near €2.4k). The exposure floor counts exposure-years and so cannot see that, and a bin holding 3,000 exposure-years may still rest on two claims. The severity view therefore applies its own minimum of 30 claims per bin on top of the floor. Burning cost keeps the exposure floor alone, since it is a cost per exposure-year, but the same large losses drive it, so read a narrow selection with that in mind.

Density is bucketed by half-decade and stored as floor(log₁₀(density) × 50) in one byte. Because a half-decade is exactly 25 codes wide, that reproduces every bucket boundary exactly, so no policy sits in the wrong bucket. Every other field is exact and no row is aggregated, sampled or rounded. Rebuild this file with .venv/bin/python dashboard/build.py.
